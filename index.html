DIY Low-Speed Wind Tunnel

Mechanical Design • Fabrication • Experimental Testing • Aerodynamics • Flow Visualization • Troubleshooting

Designed and fabricated a modular low-speed wind tunnel for qualitative aerodynamic testing and smoke-flow visualization. The system integrates flow conditioning, a contraction section, transparent test section, custom multi-port smoke rake, and variable-speed induced airflow.

Mechanical Design • SolidWorks • 3D Printing • Hardware Integration • Soldering • Testing

Retractable UAV Landing Gear

SolidWorks • Mechanical Design • Mechanisms • Engineering Drawings • Assembly Design

Designed and modeled a retractable UAV landing gear mechanism incorporating a linear actuator, linkage-driven retraction system, shock-absorbing strut, and pinned mechanical joints. The final assembly includes custom components, off-the-shelf hardware, engineering drawings, a BOM, and exploded views.
