Overview

I designed and modeled a retractable UAV landing gear mechanism in SolidWorks with an emphasis on practical mechanical design, simplified motion, lightweight construction, and manufacturability.

Mechanism Design

The assembly uses a linear actuator and linkage-driven retraction system together with pinned joints and a shock-absorbing strut. The geometry was developed to provide the required motion while keeping the design mechanically simple.

Design for Assembly

The final assembly combines custom-modeled parts with off-the-shelf hardware. Component geometry, fastener placement, joint motion, and assembly access were considered throughout the design.

Documentation

The project includes detailed engineering drawings, a bill of materials, exploded views, and collapsed/retracted assembly views to communicate how the mechanism is built and how it moves.
