Overview

I contribute to SDSU's Rocket Design and Launch Team on the propulsion side, with work centered on CAD modeling and learning from real rocket propulsion systems.

Mechanical Modeling

My work includes SolidWorks modeling of rocket-engine components and using CAD to understand how propulsion hardware is packaged and assembled.

Propulsion Research

I have studied propulsion systems including Merlin engine architecture to strengthen my understanding of practical liquid-rocket propulsion concepts.

Team Project

I contributed to the Tezcatlipoca rocket project, which achieved a 35,604.4-foot launch. As my responsibilities expand, this page will continue to document more of my direct design and propulsion work.
