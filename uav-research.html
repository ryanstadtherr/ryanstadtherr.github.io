Overview

This research project focuses on building and testing a scalable UAV platform at San Diego State University. My work emphasizes physical design, fabrication, integration, and iteration.

Mechanical Design

I have designed and refined printed and mechanical components for the platform, considering fit, mounting, motion, weight, and integration with the rest of the system.

Hardware Integration

The project includes soldering, wiring, component selection, subsystem assembly, and hands-on troubleshooting during testing.

Testing and Iteration

I helped build and integrate a subsystem enabling controlled motion in the x and y directions. Testing results are used to identify mechanical and integration issues and guide later design changes.
